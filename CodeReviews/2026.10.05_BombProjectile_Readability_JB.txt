BombProjectile.cs Code Review

Reviewer: Jordan Boogaard
Date of Review: 10/5/2026
Sprint: 2
File: BombProjectile.cs
File Author: Collin White
Time Taken: 15 minutes

Readability Comments:
- Frame sizes are in named constants and GetFrameRectangle() is short and clear
- Update() is easy to follow... fuse first, then an early return, then the explosion
- IsReturning and XDisplacement are never used (they look copied from the boomerang) and I wasted time searching for where they matter
- Velocity is set from ThrowSpeed but nothing moves the bomb, so the names suggest it travels when it doesn't
- ExplosionFrameDuration is declared but the animation uses a plain 0.12f
- Offsets like startingX + 32, startingY + 11 and the frame order {2, 1, 0, 3} have no explanation
